FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Review these frequently asked questions to discover essential details regarding admissions, specialised curriculums, and outstanding career opportunities awaiting you today.

What career opportunities are available after this programme?

Graduates can work as Solar Energy Engineer, Energy Analyst, Energy Auditor, Power Plant Engineer, Wind Farm Analyst, Energy Manager and Sustainability Consultant.

What kind of industries can students work in after completing this programme?

Graduates can work in renewable energy companies, power utilities, EV sector, smart cities, green building projects, energy consulting firms and sustainability divisions of industries.

Will this programme remain relevant in future job markets?

Yes. With India’s focus on renewable energy expansion, Net Zero targets, ESG compliance and sustainable development, demand for energy and sustainability professionals is expected to grow significantly.

Does this programme only focus on solar energy?

No. It covers renewable energy, energy efficiency, sustainability, green transport, energy economics, analytics and environmental studies.

Can students work in EV and green mobility sectors after this course?

Yes. Subjects such as sustainability, green transport and energy systems support careers in EV infrastructure and clean mobility sectors.
